# BRUSHSTEEL: how to play (human playtest)

Download `brushsteel.html` and double-click it: it opens in your browser from the computer, no install, no internet needed (you can switch Wi-Fi off). It opens on the title: draw a stroke across the name (or press Enter), then pick Duel and the first master, Oro. You duel the first master, the patient one, with the mouse. Drag across the screen to cut, drag down for an overhead, click to thrust (a ready master turns a thrust aside, so thrust right after you parry). When the master gets ready to strike, a faint ink line shows where the blow will land: cut across that line to parry, then strike back at once. A and D (or the arrow keys) step back and forward, Shift dashes, and holding S dips your brush to refill the ink meter. First to three hits wins; press R or the Restart button after a duel. Play for about 10 minutes and fill in the form below. Honest "no" answers are the most useful.

Path mode: pick "Path" in the menu (or click "Path" at the top) to play a run instead: pick a brush, then walk the day's scroll of duels, shrines (take a seal) and ink wells up to Master 8 with 3 lives. A lost duel costs a life, and so does leaving a duel before it ends. The run is saved in the browser; "End run" on the map ends it. Esc pauses a duel (Resume, Settings, Quit to menu); quitting a Path fight costs a life.

Keys: Settings > Keys (from the menu or Pause) lets you pick other keys for step, dip, dash and restart; Reset puts back A, D, S, Shift and R. The arrow keys always work too. A controller works as well (optional): the right stick draws strokes, the left stick or d-pad steps, LB or RB dashes, X dips, RT thrusts, A picks, B goes back and Start pauses.

Sound starts after your first click or key press (a controller press alone does not start it); Settings has the master volume.

Build: the single-file build of this repo (`npm run build:single` in `game/`), rebuilt whenever the game changes.
